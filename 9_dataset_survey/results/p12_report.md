## What changed from the smoke run (added by hand, 24 Nov 2026)

This is the first full p12 run (with the Lys69 fix from p21e). The 20 Nov smoke run covered our 23 only.

**Changes**

- **More proteins.** The ODC side now has 8 degraded fusions (smoke: 4; added *G. yellowstonensis*, *Porphyra*,
  *G. sulphuraria* M2YAF1 and the second *G. partita* copy A0A9C7PSW2), 41 intact fusions (smoke: 12), and a
  baseline of 34 standalone ODCs (smoke: none). The AdoMetDC side adds the 590 standalone AdoMetDCs.
- **The sign test is now nominally significant** (7 of 8 degraded proteins have interface > catalytic, one-sided
  p = 0.035; smoke: 3 of 4, p = 0.31). But two of the eight are near-copies: A0A9C7PQ51 and A0A9C7PSW2 are
  99.6 % identical (p18) with identical per-site calls here. Counting *G. partita* once gives 6 of 7, p = 0.0625.
  The result rests on that duplicate.
- **Degraded vs standalone ODC on d (interface minus catalytic): permutation p = 0.0009**, within the one stratum
  holding both groups (Rhodophyta). This is close to circular: "degraded" is defined by catalytic losses, so a
  degraded protein has a low catalytic fraction and therefore a high d almost by construction. It does not show
  that the interface is kept.

**What did not change**

- **The interface is not specially preserved.** Compare interface conservation directly: degraded fusions 0.38
  (median; range 0.30-0.46) against intact fusions 0.52 and standalone ODCs 0.59. Inside Rhodophyta, the only
  lineage-matched comparison, the degraded fusions (0.30-0.46, median 0.38) sit below the red-algal standalone ODCs
  of the same species (0.33-0.56, median 0.51; *G. partita* 0.48 and 0.51, *Rhodosorus* 0.55 and 0.56,
  *Gracilariopsis* 0.33). The degraded halves look uniformly diverged: catalytic and interface positions both lost,
  the catalytic ones more. That was the smoke-run reading too, and the baselines confirm it.
- **Degraded vs intact fusions still cannot be lineage-controlled:** no stratum holds both groups.
- **AdoMetDC half:** fusions keep slightly less of the human dimer interface than standalone AdoMetDCs (0.26 vs
  0.33 medians, permutation p = 0.085, 5 strata), and ODC status makes no difference (degraded vs intact +0.044,
  degraded vs standalone p = 0.20). No AdoMetDC-side conclusion changes.
- **Per-protein Fisher tests:** none below 0.05 (lowest *Porphyra*, 0.066).

**p20 is unchanged.** p20 reads only `p12_interface_residues.tsv` (the interface position set, unchanged except
for Lys69, which p20 already counted as catalytic, p21e). It does not use the per-protein or test tables.
