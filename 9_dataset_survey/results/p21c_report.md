## 4. Reading the result (added by hand after the run, 23 Nov 2026)

- **Red-algal fusions are not one clade**, but the break is a single sequence. They fall into two fully
  supported groups (UFBoot 100 each): the Bangiales + Gracilariales five (*Porphyra*, two *Pyropia*,
  *Gracilaria*, *Gracilariopsis*) and the *Galdieria* five. The *Rhodosorus* fusion A0AAV8UZF7 sits outside
  both, as sister to UPI0017429AE9 (UFBoot 100). The smallest clade holding all 11 red-algal fusions (UFBoot 98)
  has exactly one extra member: UPI0017429AE9.
- **UPI0017429AE9 is probably not a standalone ODC.** It (UniProt A0A6T6PB64, a TSA-derived entry) is 90.4 %
  identical over its whole 355 residues to the *Rhodosorus* fusion's ODC half (fusion residues 452-806), with no
  AdoMetDC part. It looks like a fragment of a fusion-type transcript (a second copy, or another strain's allele),
  not an independent gene. p10, p17 and p19 listed it as a *Rhodosorus* standalone ODC-family protein (3/13 sites);
  it was never counted as an intact standalone, so no p19 call changes. Treat it as fusion-derived from here on.
  With that reading, the 11 red-algal fusions plus this fragment form a clade (UFBoot 98).
- **Adding *Percolomonas* breaks the clade further**: A0A7S1KLJ4 is sister to the stramenopile *Heribaudiella*
  fusion UPI0034ACD96C (UFBoot 55, i.e. unsupported), and the smallest clade holding red algae + *Percolomonas* has
  UFBoot 66. The tree does not support red algae and *Percolomonas* together.
- **The degraded set is split across that red-algal group by sequences that simply have no call.** Inside its
  smallest clade (14 sequences, UFBoot 66) the six non-members are fusions without an AlphaFold model, so
  they could not be called degraded: *G. sulphuraria* UPI00207E5336 (99.9 % identical to the degraded M2YAF1,
  p18), the two *Pyropia*, *Gracilaria*, *Heribaudiella*, and the UPI0017429AE9 fragment. The non-monophyly of
  "degraded" is therefore mostly missing data, not interleaving with intact ODC halves: no fusion called intact
  sits inside that clade.
- ***Tistrella*** (A0A2E3ETT7, bacterium) sits among the actinobacterial PLP-first fusions (UFBoot 99), far from
  the eukaryotic degraded set; it is why the all-organisms degraded set needs a 221-sequence clade to hold it.
  That number reflects the unrooted tree (either side of a branch counts), not a meaningful group.
- **Green-lineage fusions** are nearly a clade: the smallest clade holding all 105 (UFBoot 100) has 5 extra
  members, three *Alexandrium tamarense* fusions and the tobacco ODC and *Phlegmariurus* LDC2 references.
- **Alveolata** is not close to a clade (140 intruders); dinoflagellate and apicomplexan fusions sit in
  separate parts of the tree.
- Q8IJ77 (*P. falciparum*) is sister to another *P. falciparum* isolate, as expected; it is not in any degraded
  set under the p21b core list.
- The tree is unrooted and the ODC half is about 365 aligned columns; deep nodes should be read with that in mind.
