# p18: is the degraded ODC half under purifying selection, or decaying?

Generated by `scripts/p18_dnds.py`.

## Could the question be answered?

**No.** The question needs a dN/dS for the *Gracilariopsis chorda* ODC half, and no usable nucleotide pair exists for it. Its only relatives with a fusion in p10 are *Gracilaria gracilis* and *Agarophyton chilense* (same family, Gracilariaceae); against both, synonymous sites are saturated in every region (dS > 1 or not computable). So Gracilariopsis cannot be compared with the species that have spare ODCs by dN/dS from public data. A protein-level fallback is at the end, clearly labelled; it does not answer the question.

The positive control is not usable either: the two Ceratopteris loci are saturated against each other (dS 1.1-1.8), and copies within a locus are splice isoforms of one gene. The only interpretable ratios are from *Galdieria partita* vs *G. sulphuraria*, whose ODC halves are both degraded (p10), so there is no intact-ODC-half ratio to compare them with.

**Premises in the task that do not match the project's own results** (p10_species_enzymes.tsv, p17):

- *Galdieria partita*'s standalone ODC-family proteins (A0A9C7PSP6, A0A9C7Q3X8) are 11/13, not fully intact (Y331 and D332 changed, by sequence; p10 structure 11 kept / 1 lost / 1 unresolved).
- *Percolomonas cosmopolitus*'s two ODC-family TSA proteins are 12/13 by sequence (Y331 to H or F), with no structure.
- Only *Rhodosorus marinus* has a 13/13 standalone ODC (A0AAV8UQI0, and the same protein in TSA).
