# p20: firm up or break the Galdieria ODC-half result

Generated by `scripts/p20_report.py` from the tables written by `scripts/p20_dnds_ci.py` (method in its docstring).

## Does the Galdieria ODC-half result survive?

**As a number, yes. As evidence that a dead ODC half is being kept as a structural element, no.**

- The ratio is real: ODC-half dN/dS 0.196 [0.130, 0.281], 0.196 [0.134, 0.283], 0.201 [0.136, 0.285], 0.201 [0.137, 0.280] (95 % bootstrap CI, 4 pairs, all CIs entirely below 1; no bootstrap replicate reached 1). It is below the linker in every pair (paired bootstrap p 0.008, 0.02, 0.044, 0.036).
- But intact ODC halves are at least as constrained. Across 86 unsaturated comparison pairs whose two ODC halves are both intact (part C), the ODC-half dN/dS has median 0.114 (range 0.010-0.693); 59 of 86 are below Galdieria's 0.20. A degraded Galdieria ODC half under the same constraint as a working one is what the hypothesis would predict, but it is also what an intact-like protein would show; the number does not separate the two.
- The interface pattern is not specific either. In Galdieria the interface codons are lower than the rest (part B), but the same holds in 90 of 95 intact comparison pairs (68 with p < 0.05). Interface conservation is a general property of these ODC halves, not a signature of a dead one.
- The catalytic positions are not free in Galdieria (dN/dS about 0.04 over 12 codons, CI up to about 0.5). The two species carry the same substituted residues at 5 of their 6 shared lost sites (K69R, E274N, G276D, R277S, C360G; p10_degraded_pairs.tsv), so the losses predate the G. partita - G. sulphuraria split, and the substituted residues themselves have been conserved since.
- The four Galdieria pairs are effectively one comparison (the two G. partita copies are 99.6 % identical, the two G. sulphuraria proteins 99.9 %).

## Premises that did not match the data

- PAML/codeml is not installed, so there is no likelihood-ratio test; the bootstrap is the only test.
- The 311 rows of p10_all_fusions.tsv are not 311 AdoMetDC-then-ODC fusions: 224 are AdoMetDC before the PLP domain, 64 have the PLP domain first, 23 are our set.
- The 66 bacterial rows are a different fusion: 64 have the PLP decarboxylase FIRST and a prokaryotic AdoMetDC (PF02675, not PF01536) after it, and their PLP domain is nearest the Selenomonas LDC/ODC, not human ODC. None has a structurally intact ODC half in p10 (11 incomplete, 4 undetermined, 1 degraded, 50 no model). They were still searched: 1488 bacterial candidate pairs, 1487 without a verified CDS for both members (of the 58 bacterial sequences paired: 35 no ENA protein_id in UniParc, 21 ENA translation differs from UniParc, 2 verified), 1 in the dS window, none with both ODC halves intact.
- Plasmodium, the known working bifunctional enzyme, fails the "13/13 identical" rule: 60 of its 65 fusions carry Phe at human Y331 (p10 sequence read). Its 310 congeneric pairs in the dS window were therefore not used as intact comparators. Y331F in a working enzyme means that rule is stricter than function.
- p12_interface_residues.tsv on disk is from the p12 smoke run (70 interface residues, Lys69 missing, see the p12 fix). The interface-only set used here (interface minus the 13 catalytic positions) is the same 66 residues either way.
